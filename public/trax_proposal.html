1. Executive Summary

Fayzee (fayzee.store) is a fast-growing digital e-commerce marketplace empowering manufacturers, wholesalers, and independent retail brands across Pakistan. Unlike single-merchant accounts, Fayzee operates on a centralized marketplace aggregator model, routing fulfillment orders from dozens of verified online merchants through our core platform.

To ensure superior logistics efficiency, Fayzee has fully integrated Trax API (sonic.pk) natively into our seller portal. Every registered seller on Fayzee is automatically provisioned to dispatch customer orders through Trax with 1-click doorstep pickup and real-time tracking. This document proposes a formal Tiered Corporate Partnership to establish Trax as Fayzee's preferred logistics carrier.

4. Proposed Commercial Terms & Strategic Asks

To incentivize sustained seller growth and guarantee platform exclusivity, we propose the following commercial framework:

💰 4.1. Tiered Delivery Tariff

Phase 1 Tariff: PKR 160 – 170 (0.5 KG) / PKR 190 – 200 (1.0 KG).
Growth Tier (5k+): Automatically reduces to PKR 140 (0.5 KG) upon meeting milestone volume.

⚡ 4.2. Accelerated COD Cycle (T+2)

Automated settlement within 48 hours (T+2) of parcel delivery to maintain seamless merchant payout liquidity on Fayzee.

📉 4.3. Reduced COD Fee (1.5% - 2.0%)

Reduction from standard 3.5% down to 1.5% – 2.0%, recognizing our centralized billing and zero individual acquisition cost for Trax.

🤝 4.4. Dedicated Account Manager (KAM)

Direct priority WhatsApp support channel for immediate rider coordination, daily pickups, and customer SLA escalations.
